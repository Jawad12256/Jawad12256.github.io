# Product Overview

## Purpose

This is a personal portfolio for a Design Engineering undergraduate.
It showcases projects, engineering experience, technical skills,
achievements and personal interests.

The portfolio should communicate the breadth of Design Engineering:
combining software, electronics, mechanical engineering, product
design, manufacturing, robotics and creative problem-solving.

It is also being developed for the AWS Student Builder Challenge.
The project should demonstrate technical ability and creativity
without sacrificing its usefulness as a professional portfolio.

## Target audience

- Internship and graduate recruiters
- Engineering and technology professionals
- University peers and collaborators
- Anyone interested in the author's projects and work

## Core experience

The portfolio should:
- Make the author's identity and interests immediately apparent.
- Present projects and experience in a clear, visual way.
- Allow visitors to explore detailed project information.
- Include a gallery derived from existing project and experience
  images rather than maintaining duplicate assets.
- Include an optional Minecraft-inspired Crafting Mode.
- Remain a conventional, usable portfolio even if visitors never
  interact with Crafting Mode.

## Design direction

The visual identity is dark, technical and minimal.

Use the existing design tokens:
- Background: #0a0c10
- Raised background: #12151b
- Raised hover: #181c24
- Sunken background: #06070a
- Border: #232833
- Strong border: #343b48
- Heading text: #eef1f5
- Body text: #aab2c0
- Dim text: #5b6472
- Accent: #3ee8a8
- Accent hover: #5ff0b8

Use JetBrains Mono where appropriate.

Avoid unnecessary visual clutter, excessive gradients,
generic template aesthetics and gratuitous animation.

## Product constraints

- The portfolio must work without user accounts.
- No backend or database is required.
- No AI API calls or live AI processing.
- Crafting Mode must be optional.
- All ordinary portfolio content must remain accessible without
  completing the game.
- Do not invent personal achievements, project details or claims.
  Ask for clarification where factual information is missing.
